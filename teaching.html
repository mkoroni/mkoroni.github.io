Teaching

Teaching & supervision

I teach in engineering seismology and computational wave physics, and I value supervision as a core part of research training. My teaching focuses on the link between physical theory, numerical modelling, and real-world seismic hazard assessment.

Current teaching

  • 2022–present: Lecturer for Probabilistic Seismic Hazard Assessment (MSc-level), ETH Zürich, within the Engineering Seismology programme.
  • 2018–present: Supervision of BSc, MSc, and PhD projects in computational seismology, inverse theory, waveform modelling, and physics-based ground-motion prediction.

Research themes

  • Full-waveform modelling and inversion
  • Physics-based ground-motion simulations
  • Earth structure and surface-topography effects
  • Machine learning for seismic waveform analysis
  • Probabilistic and deterministic seismic hazard
