Teaching & Supervision

I am passionate about teaching computational seismology, engineering seismology, and inverse theory. I enjoy mentoring students at all levels and supporting their research development.

Current courses

  • 2022–present: Lecturer for "Probabilistic Seismic Hazard Assessment" (MSc-level), ETH Zürich, as part of the Engineering Seismology programme. Course includes both theoretical foundations and computational practical exercises.
  • 2018–present: Supervision of BSc and MSc theses focusing on computational seismology, full-waveform modelling, physics-based ground motion, and the application of machine learning to seismic data at ETH Zürich.

Supervision Focus

  • Full-waveform modelling and inversion techniques
  • Physics-based ground-motion simulations
  • Machine learning and data-driven methods in seismology
  • Computational seismology and high-performance computing
  • Probabilistic and deterministic hazard assessment
  • Data processing and inverse theory applications
