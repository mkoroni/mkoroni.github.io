Supervision philosophy

Mentorship approach

I mentor researchers who are curious about how the Earth works, excited by rigorous methods, and willing to tackle hard problems. My supervision emphasises clear problem framing, careful validation, and scientific independence.

Methodological rigor

Sensitivity analysis, forward modelling, synthetic recovery tests, and careful validation. Know why your result matters and what could be wrong.

Clear communication

Write and speak for your audience. A paper, talk, or dissertation must be understood by someone outside your immediate field.

Research independence

My role is to guide direction and ask hard questions, not to provide answers. The goal is for you to become an independent researcher.
