Computational seismology

Maria Koroni

Senior Scientist
Swiss Seismological Service · ETH Zürich

I study how seismic waves travel through the Earth and how geological structure affects earthquake ground motion. My research combines seismic imaging, numerical wave propagation and earthquake observations to understand the physical causes of shaking and its variability.

Earth structure
and earthquake shaking

How do variations in Earth structure redistribute seismic energy? Which structural features matter for ground-motion prediction? I investigate these questions through full-waveform modelling, tomography and sensitivity analysis, with a focus on crustal structure, sedimentary basins and topography.

Research

Numerical simulations and seismic observations to investigate wave propagation in heterogeneous Earth models and the physical controls on ground motion.
