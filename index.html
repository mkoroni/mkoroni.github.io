Research Approach

Bridging seismic imaging and earthquake prediction

My research asks a fundamental question: which features of the Earth's crust must we know to predict ground motion, and why? To answer this, I combine three tools—seismic tomography, full-waveform modelling, and sensitivity analysis—to test how deep structure redistributes seismic energy and when that knowledge improves predictions.

🌍 Research

Full-waveform modelling, adjoint methods, and crustal inversion to understand how Earth structure controls ground motion in Japan and the Swiss Alps.

👥 Supervision
